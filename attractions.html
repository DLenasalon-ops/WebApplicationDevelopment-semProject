Rides & Attractions

50+ thrilling rides for every age and every kind of adventurer.

Explore Our Rides

SambFun Park features over 50 rides and attractions across 5 themed zones. Whether you are a thrill-seeker chasing the ultimate adrenaline rush, a family looking for a fun day out with the kids, or just want to cool off on a hot day — we have something for everyone.

Adventure Land

Home to our biggest coasters and thrill rides including Thunder Loop and the upcoming Sky Rocket.

Splash Zone

Water rides, wave pools, and river rapids. Bring a change of clothes — you will get wet!

Kids' Cove

A safe, supervised zone designed for children under 12 with age-appropriate rides and soft play.

Thrill Hill

Dark rides, haunted houses, and the Freefall Drop. For those who like their fun with a side of fear.

Main Street

The Safari Mini Train, restaurants, gift shops, and the starting point for all park parades.
