🎟️ Tickets & Pricing

Day pass prices: Adults (18–59) KSh 1,200 · Children (3–17) KSh 800 · Seniors (60+) KSh 600. Children under 3 enter free. Use our Ticket Calculator to get your group total instantly.

🎢 Rides & Attractions

We operate 50+ rides across 5 zones: Adventure Land, Splash Zone, Kids' Cove, Thrill Hill, and Main Street. This includes 3 roller coasters, 4 water rides, 12 family rides, and more. Sky Rocket opens July 2026!

Many rides are open to all heights. Extreme rides like Thunder Loop require 140 cm minimum. Visit our Plan Your Visit page for the full height chart.

♿ Safety & Accessibility

All rides pass 42 daily safety checks before opening. Kids' Cove is a dedicated zone for children under 12 with age-appropriate rides and supervised soft play. Children must be accompanied by an adult aged 18+.

The entire park is wheelchair accessible. We offer free wheelchair rental, companion tickets at 50% off, sensory packs at Guest Services, and quiet hours from 9–10 AM daily.

📍 Getting Here

Yes — parking is free and included with every ticket. We have 2,000 spaces on-site, including 80 accessible bays near the main entrance.

Take Route 34 (CBD → Lang'ata) and alight at the "SambFun Park" stop. Matatus run every 15 minutes from 7 AM to 10 PM.
