🎟 Tickets & Pricing

Day pass prices: Adults (18–59) KSh 1,200 · Children (3–17) KSh 800 · Seniors (60+) KSh 600. Children under 3 enter free. Use our Ticket Calculator to get your total instantly.

Yes — walk-up tickets are available at the gate every day. However, booking online saves you 10% and guarantees entry on busy weekends and public holidays.

Every Sunday we offer a Family Fun deal: 2 adults + 2 children for KSh 3,500 (saving KSh 1,100). For groups of 20 or more, contact groups@sambfunpark.co.ke for a custom quote.
🎲 Rides & Attractions

We operate 50+ rides across 5 zones: Adventure Land, Splash Zone, Kids' Cove, Thrill Hill, and Main Street. Sky Rocket — our newest coaster — opens July 2026!

Many rides are open to all heights (Carousel, Mini Train, Bumper Cars as passenger). Thunder Loop and Freefall Drop require 140 cm minimum. Visit Attractions for the full list.
♿️ Safety & Accessibility

All rides pass 42 daily safety checks before opening. Kids' Cove is a dedicated zone for children under 12 with age-appropriate rides and supervised soft play.

Yes. The entire park is wheelchair accessible. We offer free wheelchair rental, companion tickets at 50% off, sensory packs at Guest Services, and quiet hours from 9–10 AM daily.
📍 Getting Here

Yes — parking is free and included with every ticket. We have 2,000 spaces on-site, including 80 designated accessible bays near the main entrance.

Take Route 34 (CBD → Lang'ata) and alight at the "SambFun Park" stop. Matatus run every 15 minutes from 7 AM to 10 PM.
