Before You Visit

5 Things to Know

1
Check Height Requirements

Thunder Loop and Freefall Drop require a minimum height of 140 cm. Most family rides start at 110 cm. View the full chart on the Attractions page.

2
Book Online to Save 10%

Booking your tickets in advance saves you 10% and guarantees entry on busy weekends and public holidays.

3
What to Bring

Comfortable shoes, sunscreen, water bottle, and a change of clothes for water rides. Lockers are available at the entrance for KSh 100.

4
Arrive Early

The park opens at 9 AM on weekends. Arriving in the first hour means shorter queues for popular rides like Thunder Loop and Splash Canyon.

5
Free Parking & Easy Access

Free parking for 200 vehicles on-site. Matatu Route 34 stops directly at our gates every 15 minutes from 7 AM to 10 PM.
