About SambFun Park

Our story, our mission, and the people behind the magic.

Our Story

SambFun Park was founded in 2005 with a simple dream — to create a place where families could escape everyday life and experience pure joy together. What started as a small fairground with five rides has grown into one of East Africa's most beloved adventure parks, welcoming over one million visitors every year.

Our founder, Samuel Baraka, believed that laughter and adventure should be accessible to everyone. That belief still drives every decision we make today — from the rides we build to the prices we set.
