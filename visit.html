Getting Here

Easy to reach from anywhere in Nairobi.

📍 Address

SambFun Park, Uhuru Highway, off Lang'ata Road, Nairobi, Kenya.

🚗 By Car

Take Uhuru Highway southbound from the CBD. Turn left at the Lang'ata Road junction. Free parking for 2,000 vehicles on-site.

🚌 By Matatu

Take Route 34 (CBD to Lang'ata) and alight at the SambFun Park stop. Matatus run every 15 minutes from 7 AM to 10 PM.

🚕 By Taxi / Ride-share

Uber, Bolt, and Little Cab all service the park. Search "SambFun Park" in the app.

Accessibility

♿ Wheelchair Access

All zones are wheelchair accessible. Free wheelchair rental at the main entrance. Companion tickets are 50% off for visitors with disabilities.

🧏 Sensory Support

Quiet hours: 9–10 AM daily. Sensory packs (ear defenders, sunglasses, fidget tools) available free at Guest Services.

🅿️ Accessible Parking

80 designated accessible bays at the front of the car park. A covered drop-off zone is next to the main entrance.
